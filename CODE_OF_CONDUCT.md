# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to keep discussion respectful, focused, and constructive. Healthy disagreement is welcome; harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not.

## Scope

This applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions, and maintainer-led communication channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block participants who repeatedly violate this standard.

To report conduct concerns, contact the maintainer listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).
